## Purpose
The shadow camp in the Battlefield. In T2Way it gives the T2 its name (camp, then the Shadow Monk at [Menzies](/fow/duties/menzies/)).

## Objective
Kill the Shadow Monk, the Rangers and the Shadow Beast.

## Threats
- **Monk outside the ball**: it heals, the spike doesn’t kill everything.
- **Shadow Beast**: it doesn’t pop straight away; spiking before it’s in the ball leaves it untouched.

## Solutions
VoS:
1. Ball the Monk on the Rangers. Monks have a shorter range than Rangers: if the Monk gets in front of them, pull the other way.
2. Shadow Beast: stay on staff or wand + shield, no scythe.
3. Jump in, plant [Ebon Battle Standard of Honor](skill:2233).
4. Spike only when everything is balled, Beast included.
