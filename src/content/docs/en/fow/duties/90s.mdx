## Purpose
Clear the forge in pairs of groups: one pull, one spike.

## Objective
Pull the next Abyssal/Beast group into the next Monk/Mesmer group, wait for the spike, repeat. Counter-clockwise by default.

## Threats
- **Two groups at once**: the ball is too big for one spike.
- **Unannounced direction change**: the VoS waits in the wrong place.

## Solutions
- Tank: one group per pull; call any change from the default direction.
- VoS: follow the tank, pre-cast, spike as soon as the ball is tight.
