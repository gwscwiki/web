Take The Hunt from Nimros. In T2/T3Way the T1 runs clockwise around FoW (ToC, Battlefield, Burning Forest, ToS, outside Cave) to Nimros, then back to the Burning Forest; in T4way the T4 takes it. Whoever takes The Hunt also takes Slaves of Menzies from Miko.
