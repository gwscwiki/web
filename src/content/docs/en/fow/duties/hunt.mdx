## Purpose
Take *The Hunt* (Nimros the Hunter) as early as possible, together with *Slaves of Menzies* (Miko the Unchained): what follows depends on it.

## Objective
Reach Nimros, accept The Hunt, take Slaves of Menzies, head on (usually to the [Burning Forest](/fow/duties/bf/)).

## Threats
- **Distance**: in T2Way/T3Way the T1 runs clockwise (ToC, Battlefield, Burning Forest, ToS, outside Cave). Every second lost delays the team.
- **Groups dragged along to Nimros.**

## Solutions
- [Shadow of Haste](skill:929) in T2Way/T3Way, otherwise [Dash](skill:1043).
- T4way: the T4 takes The Hunt; the T1 balls or holds the first group so Rastigan can run past.
