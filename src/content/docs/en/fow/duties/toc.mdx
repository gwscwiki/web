Tower of Courage. First kill the first Ranger/Abyssal group (fast ToC: Rastigan starts moving), then all the others, and take the Vailing Lord quest. Only the ToC VoS aggroes these foes: the rest of the team runs midway without touching them.

Solo VoS: Mirage, ball, spike. Target the Abyssal of the first two Rangers and spike the groups while Rastigan runs. For the quest, pre-cast Honor and VoS when Rastigan asks to open the gate.
