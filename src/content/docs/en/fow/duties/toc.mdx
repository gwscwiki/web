## Purpose
The first quest: Rastigan has to reach the Tower of Courage. ToC also gives *The Wailing Lord*.

## Objective
1. First Ranger/Abyssal group (fast ToC): Rastigan starts walking.
2. Every other Ranger and Abyssal group.
3. Take *The Wailing Lord*.

## Threats
- **Other players’ aggro**: only the ToC VoS touches these foes; everyone else runs midway.
- **[Mirage Cloak](skill:1500) ending**: it knocks you down.

## Solutions
Solo VoS: [Mirage Cloak](skill:1500), ball, spike; [Mystic Regeneration](skill:1516) as soon as it hurts.
- **Fast ToC**: target the Abyssal of the first two Rangers, run around the tower until they ball, spike without switching target.
- **While Rastigan runs**: ball and spike the remaining groups.
- **Gate**: pre-cast [Ebon Battle Standard of Honor](skill:2233) and [Vow of Strength](skill:1759) when Rastigan asks to open it. Tank the Abyssals until Vogris uses SS on Rastigan, then strike so all three die together.
- Recast [Mirage Cloak](skill:1500) a few seconds before it ends.
