## Purpose
Menzies and his Shadow Monk (the Priest of Menzies), in the Battlefield. In T2Way the T2 chains it after [camp](/fow/duties/camp/).

## Objective
Kill Menzies and the Monk. 4-man roll-way: the text is dropped here too (“menz (drop text)”, see [text/wolf](/fow/duties/text-wolf/)).

## Threats
- **Monk standing apart**: it heals Menzies, the spike won’t land.

## Solutions
- VoS: pull Menzies and the Monk to the same side for a single ball, [Ebon Battle Standard of Honor](skill:2233), spike.
- T2Way (T2): [Sliver Armor](skill:1084) and [Ebon Battle Standard of Honor](skill:2233); [Technobabble](skill:2413) against the casters.
