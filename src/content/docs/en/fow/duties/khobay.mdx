---

Kill Lord Khobay. As a ranger: pull a group of Obsidian Furnace Drakes (unobstructed) to fuel Whirling Defense and overwhelm the group’s healer; keep the Smoke Walker away, it heals Khobay. As a VoS: keep a cover enchantment for Khobay’s Shatter, ball him with the two Warriors and spike, no Mirage.
