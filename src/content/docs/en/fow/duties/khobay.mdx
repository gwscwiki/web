## Purpose
*Khobay the Betrayer*, accepted by [south](/fow/duties/south/) after Defend. In T2Way, the T1’s last task unless the V1 does it.

## Objective
Kill Lord Khobay and his group (two Warriors, one healer).

## Threats
- **The group’s healer.**
- **Smoke Walker**: too close, it heals Khobay.
- **VoS**: Khobay’s [Shatter Enchantment](skill:69).

## Solutions
- **Ranger**: pull a group of Obsidian Furnace Drakes, unobstructed, to fuel [Whirling Defense](skill:450) and overwhelm the healer. Keep the Smoke Walker away. [Ebon Battle Standard of Honor](skill:2233) and [Finish Him!](skill:2353) to finish.
- **VoS**: one cover for the Shatter, ball Khobay with the two Warriors, spike. No [Mirage Cloak](skill:1500).
