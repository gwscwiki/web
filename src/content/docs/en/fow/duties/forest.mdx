## Purpose
Clear the forest before the griffons. Splits into [sheps](/fow/duties/sheps/), [worms](/fow/duties/worms/), [forest wolf](/fow/duties/forest-wolf/) and [bridge](/fow/duties/bridge/).

## Objective
- Every Spirit Shepherd south-west of the path.
- The 3 western Rock Borer Worms.
- The forest wolf if it has spawned.
- The [bridge](/fow/duties/bridge/) if there’s no VoS for it.

## Threats
- **Lightning damage.**
- **Forgotten bridge**: without a free VoS, nobody else does it.

## Solutions
- [weapons/shield-lightning](set:weapons/shield-lightning) and forest melee weapon; ranger: [armor/ranger-stormbound](set:armor/ranger-stormbound) optional. Basic gear is enough.
- Ranger: [Whirling Defense](skill:450).
