The griffons. For the VoS it depends on enchantment cover: count the Mesmers in the ball. With 0 or 1, cast Shards, Regen, VoS, Staggering in that order, then jump, Honor, reposition, spike. With 2, use Mirage first and let it be shattered before Honor. In T2Way, the UA pings for griffons.
