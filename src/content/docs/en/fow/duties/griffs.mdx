## Purpose
End of the run: the *A Gift of Griffons* griffons cross the area while shadow groups attack them. Dead griffons, failed quest.

## Objective
Kill every ambush (Shadow Warriors, Mesmers, Beasts) before it kills the griffons. The path must already be clear ([10 skeles](/fow/duties/10-skeles/)).

## Threats
- **Mesmers**: each [Shatter Enchantment](skill:69) strips the most recent enchantment.
- **Beasts**: [Dark Pact](skill:133) below 80% Health.
- **Warriors**: [Griffon's Sweep](skill:327); [Mirage Cloak](skill:1500) ending in their aggro = knocked down.

## Solutions
Pop the Beasts by running through the group, let them settle, count the Mesmers in the ball.
- **0–1 Mesmer**: [Sand Shards](skill:1510), [Mystic Regeneration](skill:1516), [Vow of Strength](skill:1759), [Staggering Force](skill:1498) (cover, last). ~1 s for Staggering to come back, jump in, [Ebon Battle Standard of Honor](skill:2233), reposition, spike. Cover shattered: recast Staggering before spiking.
- **2 Mesmers**: [Mirage Cloak](skill:1500) as second cover. Jump in, let Mirage be shattered **before** Honor.
- **Advanced**: without popping the Beasts. Cover for the Mesmer count, run in, [Death's Charge](skill:952) onto a caster as you drop below 80%: the Beasts Dark Pact it, not you.

Team: [Recall](skill:925) to set groups aside; T2Way: the UA pings the griffons, [Fall Back!](skill:1595) speeds up Rastigan and the griffons.
