## Purpose
*Defend the Temple of War*. Once done, south accepts [ToS](/fow/duties/tos/), [cave](/fow/duties/cave/) and [Khobay](/fow/duties/khobay/).

## Objective
Kill every Shadow Beast and Abyssal on the north and south sides until it completes. Often split: [north](/fow/duties/north/) and [south](/fow/duties/south/).

## Threats
- **No tank**, usually: each VoS holds their side alone.
- **Beasts**: [Dark Pact](skill:133); don’t tank a side below 80% Health.

## Solutions
- [Mystic Regeneration](skill:1516) and [Sand Shards](skill:1510) between waves; spike each wave as soon as it balls.
- Get back above 80% before each wave.
