## Purpose
The north side of [Defend](/fow/duties/defend/). North accepts the quest.

## Objective
Accept Defend, then kill every Beast and Abyssal on the north side until it completes.

## Threats
- **Alone, no tank.**
- **Beasts**: [Dark Pact](skill:133) below 80% Health.

## Solutions
- Cover up, let the wave ball, spike.
- [Mystic Regeneration](skill:1516) between waves; don’t engage below 80%.
