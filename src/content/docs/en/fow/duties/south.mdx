## Purpose
The south side of [Defend](/fow/duties/defend/). Afterwards, south accepts ToS, Cave and Khobay: the roles doing them are waiting.

## Objective
Kill every Beast and Abyssal on the south side, then accept [ToS](/fow/duties/tos/), [cave](/fow/duties/cave/) and [Khobay](/fow/duties/khobay/).

## Threats
- **Alone, no tank.**
- **Beasts**: [Dark Pact](skill:133) below 80% Health.
- **Forgotten quests**: ToS, Cave and Khobay are done for nothing.

## Solutions
- Same method as [north](/fow/duties/north/).
- TUF-way: the T1 pulls south out (“Pull South out”).
- Call it when the quests are taken.
