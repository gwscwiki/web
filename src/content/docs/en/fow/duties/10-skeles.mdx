## Purpose
The two skeleton groups at the Battle Field entrance stand on the griffons’ path. Alive at [griffs](/fow/duties/griffs/), they attack them.

## Objective
Kill both groups at the Battle Field entrance.

## Threats
- **Skeleton patrol**: don’t aggro it, it isn’t on the path.

## Solutions
- 2- and 3-man roll-way: the ranger, under [Whirling Defense](skill:450).
- Wait for the patrol to walk off before engaging.
