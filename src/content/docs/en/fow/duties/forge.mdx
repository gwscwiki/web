Clear (part of) the forge; depending on the strategy: left side ("forge left"), monks/mesmers ("Forge Momes"), and so on.

Solo VoS: once a tank has cleared the Abyssals and Shadow Beasts, the remaining groups of 2 Monks and 2 Mesmers can be soloed. Staggering first, then Mirage, run in, Honor, wait for 2 Shatters, recast Staggering, spike. Recast Sand Shards if needed.
