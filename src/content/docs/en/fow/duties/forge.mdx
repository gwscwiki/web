## Purpose
Clear the forge: ToS depends on it and several quests are taken there. The work is split by strategy: forge left, Forge Momes (Monks/Mesmers), [top](/fow/duties/top/), [90s](/fow/duties/90s/), [360](/fow/duties/360/).

## Objective
Abyssals and Shadow Beasts first (tank), then the groups of two Monks and two Mesmers.

## Threats
- **Mesmers**: [Shatter Enchantment](skill:69) strips the most recent enchantment; without cover, [Vow of Strength](skill:1759) goes.
- **Monks**: an incomplete spike kills nothing.
- **Beasts**: [Dark Pact](skill:133) below 80% Health.

## Solutions
Solo VoS on a Monk/Mesmer group (Abyssals and Beasts already pulled by the tank):
1. [Staggering Force](skill:1498), first cover.
2. [Mirage Cloak](skill:1500), second cover.
3. Run in, [Ebon Battle Standard of Honor](skill:2233), wait for both Shatters.
4. Recast [Staggering Force](skill:1498), spike. [Sand Shards](skill:1510) for leftovers.

Tank: [armor/assassin-vanguard](set:armor/assassin-vanguard) helps here; [Technobabble](skill:2413) when the ball won’t hold or spikes miss.
