## Purpose
The core VoS technique: follow the tank and kill their balls in one go. In T2Way the VoS spikers follow the MT.

## Objective
One ball, one spike, without losing your enchantments.

## Threats
- **Mesmers**: [Shatter Enchantment](skill:69) strips [Vow of Strength](skill:1759), [Sand Shards](skill:1510) or [Mirage Cloak](skill:1500) without cover.
- **Scythe out too early**: you pick up the hexes (Empathy, SS).
- **Three Dervishes on one ball**: two of them waste their time.

## Solutions
- Pre-cast up to 20 s ahead, especially [Sand Shards](skill:1510) and [Mystic Regeneration](skill:1516).
- Staff in hand, scythe only to strike; practise fast swaps (while sidestepping).
- Someone else tanks: jump in on staff, [Ebon Battle Standard of Honor](skill:2233), reposition, scythe, spike.
- Reposition to hit as many targets as possible rather than [Eremite's Attack](skill:1485) wherever you land; Eremite’s only after [Staggering Force](skill:1498).
- Never strip Shards, VoS or Mirage yourself.
- Consumables: at least [cupcake](cons:sweets-alcohol/birthday-cupcake), [pie](cons:sweets-alcohol/slice-of-pumpkin-pie), [corn](cons:sweets-alcohol/candy-corn); if you can, [apple](cons:sweets-alcohol/candy-apple), [egg](cons:sweets-alcohol/golden-egg), [ws](cons:other/war-supplies).
