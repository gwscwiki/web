## Purpose
Take the forge quests: without them the following objectives don’t count.

## Objective
Accept the quests at the forge as soon as they’re available.

## Threats
- **Too early**: nothing is available before the forge is done.
- **Aggro (UA)**: healing a tank or a tanking VoS pulls foes onto you.

## Solutions
- T2Way: the UA follows the MT and takes the quests.
- Roll-way: a VoS (V1 “top quests”, V2) takes them while finishing their side.
