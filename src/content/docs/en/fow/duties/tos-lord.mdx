## Purpose
The ToS lord, who finishes the Tower of Strength.

## Objective
Kill the lord.

## Threats
- **Shadow Monks**: in the lord’s range, they heal him.

## Solutions
Three options:
1. A melee stone ([legio](cons:summoning-stones/legionnaire-summoning-crystal) or similar), then [Sliver Armor](skill:1084).
2. Sliver using the Shadow Mesmers outside.
3. Pull him out and sliver with the Shadow Rangers and Monks, Monks out of his range.

Ranger: [Ebon Battle Standard of Honor](skill:2233) for damage, [Finish Him!](skill:2353) for the single target.
