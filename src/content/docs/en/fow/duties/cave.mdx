## Purpose
The Cave quest, accepted by [south](/fow/duties/south/) after Defend.

## Objective
Clear the Cave until the quest completes.

## Threats
- **Interrupts**: you get rupted easily in the Cave (T2Way/T3Way).
- **Piercing damage.**

## Solutions
- Don’t spam: cast at the right moment.
- [Heart of Shadow](skill:1032) for healing and as a fallback after a rupt.
- [armor/assassin-infiltrator](set:armor/assassin-infiltrator) optional; basic gear is enough.
