## Purpose
Clear Rastigan’s path to ToC and the team’s way through: the first move of the run.

## Objective
Aggro and keep balled, out of the way:
- the lower shadow group;
- the Shadow Elementalists;
- both Monk/Beast patrols;
- the upper group that can block Rastigan.

## Threats
- **Forgotten group**: it stays on Rastigan’s path and blocks ToC.
- **Bad spot**: other groups or patrols join the ball.

## Solutions
- Spot: the Monk/Beast spot if unsure; the high plateau south of the path once you know it.
- 5-man roll-way (T3): [Recall](skill:925) to drop the ball later without keeping aggro.
