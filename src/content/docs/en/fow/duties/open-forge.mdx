## Purpose
The forge’s first spike or first ball. ToS only starts once the forge is done: the whole run depends on it.

## Objective
Tank: ball the first group. VoS: kill it in one spike, then move on to [90s](/fow/duties/90s/) or [360](/fow/duties/360/).

## Threats
- **[Earth Shaker](skill:354)**: knocks the VoS down mid-spike.
- **Forge patrols**: they join in if you push too fast.

## Solutions
- Tank (MT4, 6-man roll-way): [Unseen Fury](skill:1041) right before the V1 spikes, against Earth Shaker knockdowns.
- [cupcake](cons:sweets-alcohol/birthday-cupcake): not before 360 is done (5-man roll-way), after the forge (6-man roll-way), so you can walk forward while pulling patrols.
- Roll-way V1: [Whirlwind Attack](skill:2107) for cleaner forge spikes.
