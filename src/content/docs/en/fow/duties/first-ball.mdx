## Purpose
T2Way: the MT’s first ball for the VoS spikers.

## Objective
A tight ball, somewhere the VoS can jump in without picking up another group.

## Threats
- **Loose ball**: the spike misses targets, survivors scatter.

## Solutions
- Plant [Ebon Battle Standard of Honor](skill:2233) as the VoS arrive.
- Call the spike early enough for them to pre-cast (see [spike](/fow/duties/spike/)).
