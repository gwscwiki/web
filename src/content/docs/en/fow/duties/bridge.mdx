## Purpose
Clear the bridge (“full bridge”) before the griffons. Without a free VoS, the forest ranger does it.

## Objective
Kill everything on the bridge.

## Threats
- **Skeletons**: slashing damage.

## Solutions
- VoS: [weapons/shield-slashing](set:weapons/shield-slashing) for the full bridge, on top of the [weapons/shield-lightning](set:weapons/shield-lightning).
- Roll-way (V3): you get there through the [backway](/fow/duties/backway/).
