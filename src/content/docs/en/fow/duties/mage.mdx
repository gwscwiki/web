## Purpose
“mage/lord (stone)”: after the forge the MT runs with the Tower Mage to ToS, then kills the lord with a stone.

## Objective
Mage at ToS, then the [ToS lord](/fow/duties/tos-lord/).

## Threats
- **Late Mage**: if nothing dies at ToS ~40 s after taking the quest, go and check.

## Solutions
- Leave with the Mage as soon as the forge is done; [Make Haste!](skill:1591) on the MT bar.
- Lord: [legio](cons:summoning-stones/legionnaire-summoning-crystal) (or another melee stone) and [Sliver Armor](skill:1084).
