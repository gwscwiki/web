## Purpose
The top of the forge, for a lone VoS.

## Objective
Solo spike the left Monk/Mesmer group, pop the Shadow Beasts near the Rangers, kill them.

## Threats
- **Mesmers**: [Shatter Enchantment](skill:69).
- **Beasts**: [Dark Pact](skill:133); don’t tank top below 80% Health.

## Solutions
- Casters: the solo VoS method from [forge](/fow/duties/forge/) (Staggering, Mirage, Honor, two Shatters, Staggering, spike).
- Beasts: pop them, let them settle, spike with cover up.
