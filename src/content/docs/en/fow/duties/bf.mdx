## Purpose
“BF”: the VoS go to the **Battle Field**, the rangers to the **Burning Forest**. *Slaves of Menzies*, taken with [The Hunt](/fow/duties/hunt/), is done in the Burning Forest.

## Objective
Burning Forest: the 5 Shadow Overlords (*Slaves of Menzies*) and the Shard Wolf.

## Threats
- **Forgotten wolf** while focusing on the Overlords.

## Solutions
- Ranger: ball the Overlords under [Whirling Defense](skill:450).
- Wolf: [Finish Him!](skill:2353), or [Ebon Battle Standard of Honor](skill:2233).
