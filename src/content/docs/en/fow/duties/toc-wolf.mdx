## Purpose
The ToC wolf. In roll-way the V2 chains it after their ToC: “toc (pop beasts, wolf)”.

## Objective
Kill the wolf and the Shadow Beasts you pop on the way.

## Threats
- **Shadow Beasts**: [Dark Pact](skill:133) below 80% Health.

## Solutions
- Pop the Beasts, ball them with the wolf, spike.
- A Dervish can split off for it during the last shadow group of *A Gift of Griffons*.
