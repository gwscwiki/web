## Purpose
Clear the forge in a single loop instead of group by group ([90s](/fow/duties/90s/)).

## Objective
Pull every Abyssal/Beast group, then kill them on top or bring the ball to a VoS.

## Threats
- **Beasts**: [Dark Pact](skill:133) on a VoS below 80% Health.
- **Running late**: in 4-man roll-way the T3 leaves before Defend (“360 (leave before defend)”).

## Solutions
- Ranger: kill the ball under [Whirling Defense](skill:450) (“360 Whirl”, TUF-way).
- Otherwise: ball to the VoS, spike.
- 5-man roll-way: no [cupcake](cons:sweets-alcohol/birthday-cupcake) until 360 is done.
