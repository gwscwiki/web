## Purpose
A fast clear on several roles’ route, right after the start.

## Objective
Kill the first skeleton group you meet.

## Threats
- **Lost time**: forge, hunt and ToC are waiting.
- **Patrol or ToC aggroed on the way.**

## Solutions
- VoS available: quick ball, spike.
- Otherwise: pull the nearby shadow group onto the skeletons.
