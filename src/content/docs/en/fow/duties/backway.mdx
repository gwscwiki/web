## Purpose
A VoS path that avoids the usual route: to the bridge in roll-way (V3, “(backway) full bridge”), to Khobay in 2-man roll-way.

## Objective
Arrive alive, then do the next duty. “Fun” 2-man roll-way: kill the already triggered shadows alone before the griffons die.

## Threats
- **Hexes** on the way.
- **Time**: in 2-man roll-way the griffons are waiting.

## Solutions
- [armor/vos-forsaken](set:armor/vos-forsaken) (Vigor + 4 Vitae) and [weapons/wand-shield-backway](set:weapons/wand-shield-backway) (quickening wand, shield with +Health while hexed).
- Swap back to scythe and usual gear before fighting.
