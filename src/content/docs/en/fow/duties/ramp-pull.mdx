## Purpose
T2Way: open the T1 and T2 routes without killing anything yourself.

## Objective
Pull the shadows onto the skeletons at the ramp and let them fight.

## Threats
- **ToC enemies**: only the ToC VoS aggroes them.

## Solutions
- Go up the ramp towards ToC, [Death's Charge](skill:952) or [Ebon Escape](skill:2420) onto a shadow above, come back down onto the skeletons.
- Once they’re in contact, break off and continue your route.
