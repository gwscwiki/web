## Purpose
The Tower of Strength, accepted by [south](/fow/duties/south/). Only starts after the forge. Usually after the Burning Forest; first in 8-man roll-way.

## Objective
1. Block the right wall, kill the 5 Shadow Patrols.
2. Kill the 10 Shadow Rangers with the Shard Wolf (same range, it balls with them).

## Threats
- **Forge not done**: wait for the enemies to spawn.
- **Nothing dies after ~40 s**: tell the team, check the Mage.

## Solutions
- Ranger: [Whirling Defense](skill:450) against the Rangers.
- [Dark Prison](skill:1044) for a faster ToS.
