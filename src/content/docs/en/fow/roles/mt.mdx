Main tank: balls groups for the VoS to spike. Usually an assassin (an R/A works in T2Way). After the forge it runs with the Tower Mage to ToS; in T2Way it stays with the team for the forest. In 6-man roll-way the slot is called "MT4" and runs an R/A.

What this role does changes from one strategy to the next: its build, duties and gear are listed above, strategy by strategy.
