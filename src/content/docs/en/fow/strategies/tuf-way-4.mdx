4-player strategy by **Ryuka [TUF]** (target time: ~12 min): three tanks and a single VoS. The T1 does the forge (open, 360, top) then south, The Hunt, Cave and Khobay; the T2 holds the first ball and does camp, Menzies and the griffons; the T3 does the forest and ToS; the VoS does ToC, the forge monks/mesmers, Defend and the griffons.

It’s the only low-man page that gives every bar’s attributes.
