One ranger and one VoS. The source gives two variants with the same bars, depending on the VoS’s skill and confidence:

- **Solo griffs** (~20 min), the "fun" one: the VoS must survive the backway to Khobay and kill the already-triggered shadows alone before the griffons die.
- **Safe and slow** (~23 min): the VoS has plenty of time.

On this site the two variants are two strategies, so each role sees the gear for its own route.
