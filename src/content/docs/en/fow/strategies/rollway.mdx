## Idea

Roll-way is FoW the way the guild [SenT] originally did it: **fast, safe runs of about 7 minutes** that don’t need perfect spawns. It’s the most played casual tactic among FoW guilds; some roles aren’t for beginners. The smaller variants (7 down to 2 players) have their own pages.

**Only the ToC VoS** aggroes the Tower of Courage quest foes (Rangers and Abyssals). The rest of the team runs **midway**: through the first ball, up the ramp toward ToC, then on to quicky without aggroing ToC — or the ToC VoS will hold a grudge.

## Before you go

The usual speedclear kit: pcons, Scrolls of Resurrection, Powerstones, one conset and summoning stones. No party-wide morale boost unless you have to.

Per-role gear comes from the same page, in three parts: **basic** (whole run), **forest** and **forge backway**. Here it’s attached to builds and duties: each role above sees the sets its duties call for. The source gives no requirement levels; "recommended" is our reading, "optional" where it says "useful but not required".

## Tasks

Every task on the routes (hold, quicky, 90s, 360, top, north/south, forest, BF…) has a duty page with the source’s definition.
