## Idea

T2Way aims for a **safe** Fissure of Woe with the end chest, friendly to new players. Two tanks (T1, T2) split the map, the MT balls groups for the spiker VoS who follow him, one VoS solos its parts, and a UA monk follows the MT, takes the quests at the forge and pings for griffons.

## What the source doesn’t give

- **MT**: the Main Tank page shows two T2way bars (one with Sliver Armor and Armor of Earth, one with Dwarven Stability and Whirling Defense) with no code or attributes, and doesn’t say which is the assassin one. So no build is attached to this role here.
- **Gear**: the role pages point to per-profession equipment pages, not covered here. The gear shown comes from the roll-way page, through the builds.

## VoS tips

Summary of the VoS page’s "How do I derv?" section:

- Consumables: at least Cupcake (running), Pumpkin Pie (attack speed) and Candy Corn (+1 attributes); if possible Apple, Egg and War Supply.
- Run with the staff, switch to the scythe only to fight.
- Pre-cast enchantments up to 20 s before spiking (Sand Shards and Mystic Regeneration especially), and reposition for a clean spike.
- If the VoS must cast Honor: jump with the staff, cast Honor, reposition, switch to scythe, spike.
- Don’t strip your own Sand Shards, VoS or Mirage; Eremite’s only after Staggering.
- In Shadow Warrior aggro, no Mirage (Griffon’s Sweep knocks down).

The source flags the VoS page as needing an update after the 2026 balance update.
