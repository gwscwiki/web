---
title: Fissure of Woe
description: "The FoW speedclear: strategies and duties."
---

Elite area that rewards obsidian shards and rare drops from the end chest. Runs usually form in Embark Beach.

FoW’s quests are largely open and non-linear, and most objectives need no special skill, so the area suits very different team splits. That’s why there are so many **strategies**, from the beginner-friendly T2Way to 2-man runs.

Low-man runs are especially popular here: a Terra ranger and a VoS dervish can do every objective safely.

Each strategy splits the team into roles (T1, MT, V1…) and gives each one its recommended build, its duties (in route order) and everything to bring: base gear, armor or weapon sets to swap in for some duties, and consumables. A role only exists within its strategy: the V1 of roll-way and the V1 of 4-man roll-way don’t do the same job.
