The UA page’s "Monk UA" bar as published, with its four open slots.

Options the source gives:

- One self-heal: usually Patient Spirit, or Healing Touch (instant, not affected by enchantment removal).
- One heal for the VoS: Dwayna’s Kiss, or Cure Hex (to remove Empathy or Spiteful Spirit).
- Two more among: Glyph of Lesser Energy (to spam Heal Party), Ebon Escape (mobility and a heal), "Fall Back!" (speed for Rastigan and the griffons; move Divine Favor points into Command), Technobabble (forge help; consider a Silencing weapon), "You Move Like a Dwarf!" (knockdown and damage).
