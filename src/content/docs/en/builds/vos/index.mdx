The Vow of Strength core: a dervish (D/–) who spikes balls with Vow of Strength, Sand Shards and Eremite’s Attack. Every VoS build starts from this bar and only changes a few slots.

Base gear and per-duty sets: from the Speedclear Wiki [Roll-way](https://wiki.gwscr.com/wiki/Rollway) page.
