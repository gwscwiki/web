The "VoS (Spiker)" bar from the VoS page, for VoS who follow the MT. Optional slot: Whirlwind Attack for cleaner forge spikes (recommended in T3/T4-way), or Mirage Cloak for survival (recommended in T2-way, against the forest foes). Stated attributes: Earth Prayers 12+1+3, Mysticism 10+1, Scythe 8+1; the published code gives Scythe 10 and Mysticism 8.
