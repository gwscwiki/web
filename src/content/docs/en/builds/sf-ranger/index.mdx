The Shadow Form ranger/assassin tank: Shroud of Distress, Shadow Form, Dwarven Stability, Whirling Defense, Heart of Shadow and Death’s Charge. The two open slots change with the route (Dash, Dark Prison, "I Am Unstoppable!", Recall, Shadow of Haste, "Finish Him!"…).

The core is this site’s construction: it groups the source’s bars by their shared skills. Gear from the Speedclear Wiki [Roll-way](https://wiki.gwscr.com/wiki/Rollway) page.
