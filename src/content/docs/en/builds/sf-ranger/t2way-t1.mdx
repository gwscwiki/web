The T1 page’s bar: two optional slots, one of which is Shadow of Haste (to take The Hunt in T2Way/T3Way) in the published code. Attributes: Expertise 12+1+3, Shadow Arts 12, Marksmanship 3.

Options the source gives:

- Shadow of Haste: for The Hunt in T2Way/T3Way.
- Dash: general mobility.
- Dark Prison: faster ToS and mobility.
- Honor (Ebon Battle Standard of Honor): damage on the ToS lord and Khobay, and helps the VoS spike in some tactics.
- "Finish Him!": single targets (ToS lord, wolves, Khobay, leftovers).
- "I Am Unstoppable!" or Dark Escape: survival (recommended for beginners).
