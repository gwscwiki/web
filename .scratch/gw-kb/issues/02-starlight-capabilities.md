Type: research

## Question

Can current Astro Starlight do what this site needs, and how? Specifically: French as the root locale with English under `/en/`; fallback to the other locale with a "not yet translated" notice; custom content collections (YAML/JSON data and MD/MDX) alongside the docs collection, with pages generated from them; Pagefind search covering French; overriding/adding page components (skill bar, gear card, duty badge); autogenerated vs hand-written sidebar; image optimisation for screenshots and handling of short video loops; deploying to GitHub Pages. Note any limits that would push toward plain Astro instead.
