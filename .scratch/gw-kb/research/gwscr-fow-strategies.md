## Roll-way equipment (only page that lists gear)

General: PCons, Scrolls of Resurrection, Power Stones, 1 conset, summoning stones. Avoid party-wide morale boosts unless needed.

- VoS basic: 5× Windwalker, sup Vigor, sup Earth Prayers, minor Mysticism, 1× Clarity/Restoration/Vitae.
  q9 scythe (20% ench, 15^ench "Guided by Fate", zealous/vamp); staff (20% ench, +5 armor, 10% cast); longbow.
  Stones: Legionnaire, Ghastly, Mystical (warhorns, tengus).
- VoS forest: shield (+45^ench, +10 vs lightning); shield (+45^ench, +10 vs slashing, for full bridge); melee weapon (20% ench, 10% cast).
- VoS forge backway: 5× Forsaken, sup Vigor, 4× Vitae; wand (10% HRC, quickening); shield (+60^hex, -3^hex).
- Ranger basic: 5× Blessed, sup Vigor, sup Expertise, 1–3× Vitae/Restoration/Recovery. Caster weapon 20% ench +5 energy (Silencing spear if Technobabble); shield +45^ench +10 vs piercing; caster staff 20% ench 10% HCT 10% HCT; optional high-energy staff; longbow; low-req daggers (20% ench, vamp, +15^ench/stance).
- Ranger forest: melee weapon 20% ench +5 energy; shield +45^ench +10 vs lightning; full Stormbound armor useful, not required.
- Assassin basic: 5× Blessed, sup Vigor, sup Shadow Arts, 1–3× Vitae/Restoration/Recovery. q9 earth staff (20% ench, +5^ench, +5 energy or HCT); caster weapon (Silencing spear); shield +45^ench +10 vs piercing; longbow; q9 daggers (20% ench, vamp, +15^ench). Optional: full Infiltrator's (cave), full Vanguard's (forge).

## Roll-way task glossary (paraphrased)

- Midway: run through the first ball, up the ramp to ToC, then on to quicky without aggroing ToC (only the ToC VoS may aggro ToC mobs).
- Hold / hold 1st ball: aggro the lower shadow group, the shadow elementalists, the two monk/beast patrols and the upper group that can block Rastigan; pull them to a safe spot.
- Quicky / quick ball: kill the first skeleton group, with a VoS or the nearby shadow group.
- 10 skeles: kill the two skeleton groups at the Battle Field entrance that would block the griffons later; don't aggro the patrol.
- ToC: first kill the first Ranger/Abyssal group (Rastigan starts moving), then the rest, and take the Vailing Lord quest.
- 90s: in forge, pull each Abyssal/Beast group into the next Monk/Mesmer group for a VoS spike, group by group, counter-clockwise by default.
- 360: in forge, pull all Abyssal/Beast groups; kill them on top or ball them for a VoS.
- Top: in forge, solo spike the left Monk/Mesmer group, pop the Shadow Beasts near the Rangers, and kill it.
- North / South: the two sides of Defend the Temple of War. North takes Defend; South takes ToS, Cave and Khobay once Defend is done.
- Forest: kill the Spirit Shepherds south-west of the path, the western three Rock Borer Worms and the forest wolf; clear the bridge if no VoS is free.
- BF: VoS go to the Battle Field, Rangers to the Burning Forest.
